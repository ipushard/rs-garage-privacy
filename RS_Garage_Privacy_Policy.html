RS Garage Service Records - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

RS Garage Service Records is a classroom prototype designed to help a garage manage customers, vehicles, service history and maintenance reminders.

Information stored

The application can store customer names, optional company information, phone numbers, email addresses, vehicle details such as year, make, model, licence plate, VIN and mileage, and service information such as service dates, service type, cost, status, notes and next-service dates.

How information is used

The information is used only to display and manage garage service records, maintain vehicle service history, and prepare maintenance reminders.

Storage and transmission

Data is stored locally on the computer running the application in text files. The prototype does not use cloud storage, analytics, advertising networks, tracking services, or a remote customer database.

Email reminders

The application can prepare a reminder email using the computer's default email application. RS Garage Service Records does not automatically send email and does not operate its own email server.

Privacy acceptance

The application records whether the privacy policy has been accepted so the user does not need to accept it on every launch. Declining the policy closes the application.

Data deletion

Customer, vehicle and service records can be deleted from within the application. Because this is a local prototype, the local text files can also be removed from the application folder to clear stored information.

Children

This application is intended for garage/service-business staff and is not designed for children.

Contact

For this classroom prototype, privacy questions can be directed to the project developer through the course submission contact information.
